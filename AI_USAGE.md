# AI Usage Guidelines

## Purpose

Talent Prism demonstrates how artificial intelligence can be integrated into applicant intake and evaluation workflows.

The AI components included in this repository are intended to assist recruiters by automating repetitive evaluation tasks and providing structured decision-support information.

AI should augment human judgment—not replace it.

---

# Intended Use

The reference implementation demonstrates AI-assisted workflows such as:

- Photo quality assessment
- Candidate profile analysis
- Structured scoring
- Evaluation summaries
- Recommendation generation

Organizations are encouraged to customize prompts, scoring methodologies, and evaluation criteria to suit their own operational requirements.

---

# Human Oversight

All AI-generated outputs should be reviewed by qualified human reviewers before making recruitment or business decisions.

Organizations should never rely solely on AI-generated recommendations.

Human reviewers remain responsible for:

- Final applicant evaluation
- Recruitment decisions
- Policy compliance
- Ethical review
- Quality assurance

---

# Model Limitations

Large Language Models and multimodal AI systems may:

- Produce inaccurate information
- Misinterpret images
- Miss important context
- Produce inconsistent outputs
- Exhibit bias
- Change behavior following model updates

AI outputs should always be validated before use.

---

# Prompt Customization

Organizations are encouraged to:

- Review prompts
- Modify evaluation criteria
- Define organization-specific scoring
- Adjust qualification thresholds
- Continuously evaluate AI performance

The prompts included in this repository are examples rather than universal best practices.

---

# Fairness and Bias

Organizations deploying Talent Prism should regularly evaluate AI performance for:

- Fairness
- Bias
- Consistency
- Transparency
- Explainability

AI-assisted systems should support equitable recruitment practices.

---

# Privacy

Organizations should ensure that applicant data processed by AI services complies with all applicable privacy and data protection regulations.

Sensitive information should only be shared with AI providers when appropriate safeguards are in place.

---

# Responsibility

Organizations deploying Talent Prism remain solely responsible for:

- AI configuration
- Prompt engineering
- Model selection
- Output validation
- Regulatory compliance
- Recruitment decisions
