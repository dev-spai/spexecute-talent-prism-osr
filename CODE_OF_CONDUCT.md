# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone participating in the Talent Prism community.

We believe that open-source projects thrive when contributors feel comfortable sharing ideas, asking questions, providing feedback, and collaborating professionally.

Participation in this project should be a positive experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or location.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Being respectful and professional.
- Being open to constructive feedback.
- Assuming good intentions.
- Sharing knowledge and helping others learn.
- Focusing discussions on technical topics and project improvements.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Providing constructive code reviews and feedback.
- Communicating with patience and courtesy.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Abusive, threatening, or intimidating language.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private or confidential information without permission.
- Spam or unsolicited advertising.
- Repeated off-topic discussions that interfere with project collaboration.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered unprofessional or harmful to the community.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Documentation
- Community forums
- Any official communication channels related to Talent Prism

---

# Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers.

When reporting an incident, please include as much relevant information as possible so the situation can be reviewed fairly.

Reports will be handled respectfully and, where practical, confidentially.

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate these standards.

Depending on the severity of the violation, maintainers may take actions including:

- Requesting changes to behavior.
- Removing inappropriate content.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently banning individuals from participating in the project.

---

# Community Expectations

Talent Prism exists to encourage collaboration, learning, and knowledge sharing.

Healthy technical debate is encouraged.

Personal attacks are not.

When disagreeing with others, focus on ideas rather than individuals.
