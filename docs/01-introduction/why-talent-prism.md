# Why Talent Prism?

## The Revenue Engine for Talent Discovery

Most agencies measure the ROI of their software by calculating administrative hours saved. In the talent industry, that is the wrong metric.

Saving a recruiter 40 hours a month is nice. But finding the next $250,000 campaign face before your competitors do is what actually scales an agency.

Talent Prism Lite is an open-source, AI-assisted evaluation engine designed for one purpose: to ensure your agency never misses a superstar because they were buried at the bottom of an inbox.

### The Economics of the Missed Superstar

In modeling and casting, revenue follows a strict power law.

The Baseline: An average working model brings in $10,000 to $30,000 in agency commission annually.

The Breakthrough: A single breakout talent generates $150,000 to $500,000+ in revenue, elevating your agency's brand and opening doors to top-tier client retainers.

If your agency receives 1,000 to 2,000 digital open-call submissions a month, 98% of them are noise. The problem is that your next breakthrough model is sitting at application #842.

When your scouts have to manually click through 800 unqualified portfolios, reviewer fatigue sets in. They start skimming. They make split-second rejections. And eventually, they miss the 1-in-1,000 submission that could have changed your business.

The true cost of a manual intake process isn't the hourly rate of your scout. It is the $250,000 in lost commission when that missed model signs with a rival agency.

### Desk Work vs. Real-World Scouting

Top scouts are valuable because of their eye, their network, and their ability to convince talent to sign. They are not valuable when doing data entry.

Every hour a scout spends at their desk sorting through bad iPhone photos in a spreadsheet is an hour they are not out in the real world—at events, shows, or on the street—finding unrepresented talent.

### Talent Prism Lite flips this dynamic:

Automated Triage: It instantly filters out submissions that don't meet your base criteria (height, location, media quality).

AI Pre-Scoring: It evaluates the visual media against your agency's specific aesthetic markers.

The 5% Rule: It routes only the absolute highest-probability candidates to your scouts' dashboard.

Your scouts stop managing inboxes and go back to doing what makes you money: evaluating the best and scouting the streets.
